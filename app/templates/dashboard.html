      Google OAuth Setup Required

      Direct Google OAuth 2.0 connection allows the agent to synchronize your calendar and use your Google profile. To enable this, configure Google Cloud credentials in your .env file.

      Step-by-Step Instructions:

      1. Open Google Cloud Console → Credentials.

      2. Create OAuth Client ID (Application type: Web application).

      3. Set Authorized redirect URI to:

      http://localhost:8000/auth/google/callback

      4. Add into your .env file:

      GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID=your_client_id_here
GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET=your_client_secret_here

      5. Restart the server.

      No OAuth Keys? Sign In Directly

      You can also sign into Google directly inside your user-isolated Chromium session.
